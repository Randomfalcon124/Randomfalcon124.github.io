Hardpoint Robotics

Co-Founder · Jun 2026 – present · Austin, TX

Hardpoint builds carbon fiber drone airframes in the United States for labs, programs, and contractors who cannot fly foreign-sourced hardware. Every frame is built to be taken apart: arms, motor mounts, and landing gear legs unbolt in minutes, so a hard landing costs one part instead of the whole aircraft.
